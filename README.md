### MeeTune, your music side (beta)
One app for YouTube videos **and** YouTube Music. Tap the logo on Home and MeeTube turns into a music app; tap it again to go back.

- 🎤 **AI Karaoke (beta):** removes the vocals from any song right on your phone, so you can sing along. Nothing is uploaded.
- 🎧 **MeeTuneMix:** songs flow into each other like a DJ set. The ending song fades out where its music ends and the next one fades in, with no volume jumps or cut vocals.
- **Your music:** YouTube Music's Home, Explore, Library and Search, with albums, artists, playlists, moods and your liked songs.
- **A real music player:** synced lyrics, a moving cover, the song's format on a glass pill, and a radio that keeps the songs coming.
- **Sound that surrounds you:** Spatial sound with a real head model (MIT KEMAR) and a Cinema level with deeper bass, Head tracking on Android 13+ through your phone's own spatial audio, Dolby, headphone correction, and crossfade between songs.
- **Quality your way:** pick the audio quality for Wi-Fi and for mobile data, and a speed for your songs (0.25x to 4x, in 0.01 steps) that a video's speed never changes.
- 🚗 **Android Auto:** MeeTune in your car with Home, Made for you, Recents and Library, voice search and lyrics. Tap **Connect to car** in the player menu to move the song you're playing to the car.
- **Plays anywhere:** downloads with lyrics play offline, and cached songs play with no network at all.
- **Its own space:** a History, Downloads and an equalizer just for your songs, and a player kept apart from your videos.

MeeTune is in beta and growing with every release.

## Build

```
./gradlew :mobile:assembleStmobileDebug     # debug, for testing
./gradlew :mobile:assembleStmobileRelease   # release, signed with keystore.properties if present
# APKs: mobile/build/outputs/renamed_apks/stmobile<Type>/MeeTube_<version>_<abi>.apk
```

Releases are made by pushing a `vX.Y.Z` tag: [release.yml](.github/workflows/release.yml) builds the signed APKs and publishes the GitHub release with its changelog. The glass is `mobile/src/stmobile/java/com/newtube/mobile/ui/common/GlassView.java`; the README showcase comes from `python tools/render_showcase.py`. Clone with `--recurse-submodules`. The backend lives in the `MediaServiceCore` and `SharedModules` submodules (MeeTube's own forks). Add `-PemulatorAbi` to run on an x86_64 emulator. Developer notes are in [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md).
