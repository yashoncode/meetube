# MeeTune tablet testing audit

## Summary verdict

MeeTune can browse and play real music on this tablet, but its UI does not yet meet issue #254's tablet-quality expectation. The most disruptive problems are oversized album/artist/playlist headers, dimensions retained from the previous orientation, the absence of a useful landscape player arrangement, and player sheets disappearing on rotation.

The audit records **6 major and 7 minor findings, with no blocker or app crash observed**. Two major findings, stale song filtering and the signed-out Library gate, affect basic functionality beyond tablet layouts. Switching back to MeeTube deliberately pauses music and is recorded separately from successful continuity within MeeTune.

## Environment and method

- Date: 2026-11-16.
- App: `io.yash.meetube`, installed version `3.5.0 Drago`, versionCode `30500`, targetSdk `37`.
- Device: **emulator-5558 only**, Android 16, 1600 x 2560 px portrait / 2560 x 1600 px landscape, 320 dpi, approximately 800 x 1280 dp, sw800dp.
- Scope: `com.newtube.mobile.ui.meetune.MeeTuneActivity`, its music pages, settings, music player and shared dialogs reached from music. Video feeds, video watch pages, comments and video channel pages belong to the separate video audit.
- Tested the installed APK. No Gradle, build, install, or source modification was performed. Local source at `b24a0604015302b88fc7a3760aa4a01fe656f4fe` was read to identify likely causes; correspondence between that checkout and the installed APK was not independently established.
- Used UI taps, scrolls, slow 1100 ms seek drags, UI Automator hierarchies, screenshots, media-session state and per-area logcat checks. Rotated while each accessible page/dialog was open, rather than testing only fresh launches.
- Default theme was Dark. Also exercised Light Home, Search and the full player in both orientations. Restored Dark and the original hardware-keyboard IME setting (`show_ime_with_hard_keyboard=0`). Equalizer remains Off, with its original Dynamic mode restored. Folder selection was cancelled.
- Playback observations use advancing elapsed time, changing lyrics, transport behavior and media-session state. There was no independent audio recording or subjective sound-quality evaluation. Some hierarchy captures deliberately paused and resumed music to let UI Automator reach idle.
- Evidence is in [build/tablet-audit/meetune](../../build/tablet-audit/meetune). Use the linked captures below as the evidence index; exploratory capture names do not always describe the final visible screen. UI dump XML and per-area `*-exceptions.log` files accompany the screenshots.

Users open music by tapping the MeeTube brand pill/icon. MeeTune's brand returns to MeeTube; its Settings also exposes a MeeTube link. See `mobile/src/stmobile/java/com/newtube/mobile/ui/browse/BrowseTopBar.java:30` and `mobile/src/stmobile/java/com/newtube/mobile/ui/meetune/MeeTune.java:86`.

## What already works well on tablet

- **Real playback and rotation continuity inside MeeTune work.** Paparazzi advanced from 0:06 in [07](../../build/tablet-audit/meetune/07-player-portrait.png) to 0:24 in [08](../../build/tablet-audit/meetune/08-player-landscape.png). The final queue selection Rasputin progressed from **0:51 portrait to 0:57 landscape** in [153](../../build/tablet-audit/meetune/153-final-playing-portrait.png) and [154](../../build/tablet-audit/meetune/154-final-playing-landscape.png). Browsing, settings and returning to mini player retained the active song.
- Music loading, album/artist/public-playlist browsing, search results, automatic related playback, Previous/Next, seek, lyrics and queue selection are operational. A portrait seek moved to 1:57; the landscape seek also registered using a slow drag.
- Synced lyrics are present and readable, with state retained through rotation. Text generally remains readable at the default font size; large transport buttons are easy to hit.
- Horizontal album and mood shelves reveal more items in landscape without inflating every card. Account and Sort popups follow their anchors correctly, unlike the fixed-position song popups.
- Android Share and folder pickers use bounded tablet surfaces and reflow. No obvious status-bar/taskbar collision was seen on browsing/settings. The landscape player intentionally becomes immersive, while the portrait player respects the visible bars.
- Light-theme Home/Search and mini-player colors adapt correctly; theme switching did not produce an app crash.

Playback continuity across the mode switch is the exception: [112 media state](../../build/tablet-audit/meetune/112-before-switch-media.txt) records PLAYING for Obsessed, and [113 media state](../../build/tablet-audit/meetune/113-after-switch-media.txt) records PAUSED for the same track. This is consistent with F13's source.

## Crash checks and limits

After each area, logcat was filtered as binary-safe text with `grep -a -E 'FATAL|AndroidRuntime|Exception'`. Captured checks cover Home, playback/queue, lyrics, album, artist, playlists, search, Library/sign-in, Explore, settings/quality, equalizer/folder, song menus, Sleep/offset, Cast, switching, Light, moods and Share.

No captured log showed a MeeTune fatal exception, app-process crash or ANR. Most AndroidRuntime lines came from the UI Automator command itself. System launcher/XR/ethernet/Bluetooth exceptions and recoverable network connection exceptions were present. Successful subsequent playback distinguishes those from an observed music crash. Representative logs: [settings](../../build/tablet-audit/meetune/settings-exceptions.log), [search](../../build/tablet-audit/meetune/search-exceptions.log), [Light](../../build/tablet-audit/meetune/131-light-complete-exceptions.log), [final playback](../../build/tablet-audit/meetune/154-end-exceptions.log).

Not validated: authenticated Library collections or account switching, successful Likes/Save to playlist, download completion/offline playback, a physical Cast receiver, system equalizer sound changes, accessibility service traversal, process death, split-screen/multi-window or larger font scaling. No account credentials or receiver were available. No download/delete/cache-removal action, timer, log sending or outbound sharing was performed. These are coverage limits, not passes.
